about

I'm an undergraduate in Biomedical Engineering at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University. I build and test deep learning methods for brain MRI, mostly deformable registration and auditing what medical imaging models actually learn.

I'm drawn to what a headline accuracy number leaves out: a deformation field that folds, a classifier that leans on shortcuts, attention that misses the lesion. I prefer controlled, reproducible comparisons, and I publish per-subject results alongside the code.

Undergraduate Research: Interpretable Tumor Classification

Feb – Jul 2026

NYCU Institute of Brain Science · Prof. Li-Fen Chen

  • MLCN 2026 @ MICCAI 2026 · publishedDesigned a model-agnostic, retraining-free audit that checks a brain tumor MRI classifier at three levels: how its feature space is organized, whether its features align with radiomic descriptors, and whether its attention falls on the tumor.
  • Fine-tuned ResNet-50 with patient-disjoint five-fold cross-validation (96.46 ± 1.39% accuracy) and audited out-of-fold features: diagnostic clustering reached ARI 0.904, and the share of features strongly aligned with radiomic descriptors rose from 5.6% to 24.5%.
  • Shifted-input controls showed that attention follows meningioma and pituitary tumors when they move off-center, while glioma attention stays diffuse.
